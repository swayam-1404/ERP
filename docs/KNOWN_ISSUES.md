# Known issues / pending scope

- The 16-module production ERP is incomplete. Current validated scope is the institutional/student/faculty development increment described in README.
- Remaining Phase 2: campuses, semester configuration, permission/role assignment administration, notices and approval workflows. MFA setup/sign-in implemented; recovery codes/administrator recovery procedures pending.
- Remaining Phase 3: configured official eligibility, secure documents, seat allocation, delegated authority and refund/withdrawal handling. Intake, independent evidence review, configured fee plans, separate payment verification, final approval and atomic enrollment conversion are implemented. These are manually verified external references, not automatic official eligibility/bank/SAMS validation. Applicant self-service, enquiries, reopening/resubmission and full admissions reports remain pending. Production faculty provisioning/full workload, transfers, scholarships, subject registrations and complete history also remain. Faculty private applicant/student/finance access is denied.
- Timetable/attendance supports dated periods and weekly series with manual skips, advisory conflict preview, atomic creation and future cancellation. Remaining Phase 4: template editing, practical/lab workflow, subject registration, approved shortage alerts, student/guardian visibility and rescheduling. Saved rosters are snapshots; future enrollments are not automatically added. Later phases (exams/finance/certificates/additional modules/mobile/deployment) not implemented. No disconnected fake module pages.
- Official current academic/attendance/fee/eligibility/certificate/signature policies remain unconfigured. Historical VIT 2025–26 fee tables and an undated conflicting disclosure were found; source years/amounts are documented separately and cannot automatically activate for 2026–27. VDC +3 uses an external-payment/selection guard backed by its published guidance. Reference advertisements are not active programme approvals. VIT source priority/postcode separation and VHSS exclusion are operational decisions, not asserted institutional corrections.
- Finance is an admission subledger only. No live gateway, bank/SAMS integration, statutory receipt numbering, refunds, reversals or general ledger. Confirmed/pending payments block cancellation until a future refund/withdrawal workflow exists. Void charges are retained and cannot be reissued on the same application. Existing direct student maintenance is not a paid-admission approval path.
- Native PostgreSQL 17.11 is the tested local fallback. Docker daemon did not start; production images/services remain unverified. Flutter 3.41.6 was found but no mobile project/device tests run.
- Local startup and existing-data idempotent seeding tested; complete clean-machine initializer and guarded reset not rehearsed. Reset script predates teaching/admission dependencies and needs updated dependent-table cleanup and isolated rehearsal before use. Do not run it on the current dataset; its transaction will otherwise encounter foreign keys. It removes development audit history and must never target live data.
- RLS is a proposed additional defense, not implemented. Current enforced isolation uses authenticated assignments, policy checks, scoped queries and composite FKs, with executed rejection tests.
- Secure uploads/S3, Hangfire/SignalR workflows, off-server backups, actual restore, signature licensing and performance/accessibility audits remain pending.
- Production web theming/messaging and real role onboarding require a later release pass. Infrastructure files are preparation, not validated deployment.
- Public staff references never create privileged users; all current seeded accounts/faculty are fictional. Brand logos/photos/documents were not republished.
