Required before official calculation: regulator/university rule document, publication/effective date, programme/batch applicability, subject credits, component maxima and minima, pass requirements, grade bands, rounding, SGPA/CGPA formula, backlog treatment, improvement rules, result approval authority and amendment policy. Rules are immutable versions once used; corrections create audited revisions. Missing official rules block official publication and eligibility decisions. DEMO rule sets may exercise workflows, visibly labelled DEMO. No assumed 75% attendance rule or invented fee table.

Certificates require written institution/type authority, template approval, serial format, signing authority and licensing review. University degrees remain externally issued records. Neither a PDF nor a QR code establishes legal signing authority.
