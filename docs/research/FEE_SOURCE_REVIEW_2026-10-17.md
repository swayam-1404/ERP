# Fee source follow-up — 2026-10-17

User directed fee research to VHEI and its individual institutions. This supplements the original 34-source/99-fact discovery snapshot; it does not silently revise that snapshot or seed active charges.

| Reference | Source | Finding / applicability |
|---|---|---|
| FEE-001 | [VIT programme fee table](https://www.vitbargarh.ac.in/pages/program-fees-structure) | Official page and rendered tables inspected. Both blocks explicitly say 2025–26. First block: day-scholar terms INR 31,000/25,000, annual 56,000; residential terms 72,000/64,000, annual 136,000. Second block: day-scholar 43,500/37,500, annual 81,000; residential 84,500/76,500, annual 161,000. B.Tech/MBA tab labels appear together; do not infer programme/batch applicability or activate for 2026–27 without review. |
| FEE-002 | [VIT mandatory disclosure](https://vitbargarh.ac.in/Updated%20Mandatory%20for%20B.Tech.pdf) | Public extraction lists INR 45,000 annually and boarding/lodging INR 30,000 per semester. Effective academic year is not established here. This differs from FEE-001; retain both and require applicability review. |
| FEE-003 | [VDC admission guidance](https://vdcbargarh.ac.in/home/admission_rules) | UG selection follows SAMS, with online admission payment and document verification. It does not publish a numeric current tuition schedule. +3 plans therefore require ExternalPortal mode and final approval requires a selection/intimation reference. This records human verification of external evidence, not a SAMS integration. |
| FEE-004 | [VDC 2025 examination notice](https://www.vdcbargarh.ac.in/uploads/images/notice/%2B3_4th_Sem_2025_Form_fill_up.pdf) | Examination-specific historical rates; not an admission tuition schedule. No automatic admission charge. |

VHEI central site and VPT/VNC/VPC subdomains returned JavaScript shells through the web reader. VSBM/VLS subdomains were unavailable through that reader; targeted domain searches returned no fee results. This is not evidence that no fee schedules exist. Current year-specific numeric schedules for those institutions remain unverified. VCPS has no independently verified institutional site in the initial inventory.

Implementation: no official amount is automatically activated. A plan records programme, session, printed source year, amount, source and admission authority reference; another administrator approves it, and the source year must match the selected session. This is a manual approval control, not an assertion that any entered reference is official. All local demonstration payments/plans are explicitly synthetic. Discounts, one-time/yearly breakdowns, concessions, refunds, transport/hostel schedules and automated official policy validation remain pending.
